Straight answers to what you're searching

The questions teams (and AI assistants) ask us most, answered simply.

Q. Will I get a real person, or a bot and a portal?

A real person, every time. The portal and API are there if you want them, but you never have to use them. Call, email or message us however you like and you'll get straight through to the same team who know you.

Q. What happens if something goes wrong with a delivery?

A named person is already on it and keeps you updated directly: no ticket queue, no chasing, no hold music. You'll always know it's being handled.

Q. Is there a same-day courier near me?

Yes. Cal runs a nationwide network of 10,000+ couriers with hubs in the North (Birchwood) and South (Devizes), and a 45-minute average collection time, 24/7/365.

Q. Can you deliver urgent pallets or freight same-day?

Yes. Our haulage network runs 7.5t to 40ft trailers with tail-lift, curtainside, HIAB and Moffett options for palletised, loose or oversized loads.

Q. Do you handle white-glove, two-person installs?

Yes, with site surveys, specialist equipment, delivery to the room of choice, and installation or waste removal where needed.

Q. Can Cal integrate with my systems or give me an API?

Yes. Book through a live portal, our REST API or webhooks, and connect Cal to your WMS, ERP, HubSpot, Xero or Zapier, with approval workflows on top.

Q. Is same-day cheaper than the pallet network?

For local, direct and time-critical jobs, often yes: one dedicated vehicle with no depots or sortation frequently beats pallet-network rates.

Q. Do you carry medical, pharma and high-value goods?

Yes. We move sensitive, high-value and regulated goods for healthcare, pharma, supply-chain and telecoms clients, on dedicated vehicles with full audit trails.
