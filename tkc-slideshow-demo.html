Automated SMS communication
Assign the team. The messages send themselves.
The instant a fitter and two drivers are on the same job, everyone gets confirmed — and everyone gets each other's number.
  • 📇 Contact-sharing — once 2+ roles are on a job, phone numbers exchange automatically. No more "does anyone have the driver's number?"
  • 🚨 Two-man notice — every message states clearly whether it's a Two-Man Team or a Two-Man Assist, and what that means on site.
  • 🔁 Replayable — hit replay to run the sequence again for the room.
