04The opportunity

How the
partnership works.

Our model lets you build and run your own company inside the Cal Group, so you get the independence of ownership alongside the reputation, systems and scale of an established business.

Equity is usually set at around 30%, based on your experience and the value you bring. From there your reward comes two ways. You draw a salary plus dividends as the business performs, and you build capital value in the shares you hold.

Within the business, the big calls are yours. You choose the market you go after, you shape how it grows, and you decide how to invest, all at the pace you set.

06Cal South

From corporate director
to business owner.

In 2023, Paul Gisbourne stepped away from a successful corporate logistics career to chase the one thing it could never offer him: ownership. Rather than open a regional office, Cal built Cal South Limited around Paul and paired his leadership with the strength of the wider group.

What appealed to him wasn't a bigger title. It was the freedom to choose the sectors and customers he worked with, and the room to act on his own judgement about investment, systems and growth, quickly and without red tape. With a candid, committed partnership from Cal behind him, he built the business from nothing.
