How the mileage & grouping work
Distances are computed instantly, offline: road miles ≈ straight-line × 1.3 (a standard UK approximation), drive time ≈ 32 mph average — so nothing depends on a live map server that could hang. The May sample's locations are built in; your own uploads are geocoded with postcodes.io. Each vehicle's stop order is optimised for the fewest miles as an open path — depot out to the last drop, no return leg (you don't drive back to base) — using 2-opt and Or-opt. To decide the split, the tool runs several different grouping strategies against each other — nearest-first, farthest-first, biggest-margin-first, biggest-drop-first, and a savings/merge method — then polishes each with a local search that shuffles stops between vehicles and DX and rescues profitable clusters. Whichever strategy yields the biggest saving vs DX wins that day; different days are won by different strategies. Every route kept is cheaper than DX and fits the working day.
