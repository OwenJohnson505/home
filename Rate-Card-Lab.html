How the maths works. Each vehicle class has a driver cost model fitted from job history: a flat driver minimum for short work, then a linear cost per mile (weighted fit on 5 to 10 mile band medians, +3% recency uplift). Blended margin weighs your card against the region's own mileage mix, with any single lane capped at 60 jobs so contract shuttles cannot steer the result. Central London collections, multi-drop contract tariffs, temp-controlled and 2-man work are excluded. Classes marked thin data borrow shape from the North fit where their own volume is not enough.
